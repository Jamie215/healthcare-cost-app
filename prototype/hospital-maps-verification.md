# Google Maps hospital verification — September 18, 2026

The original 25 Ontario entries were cross-checked against Google Maps. The directory now contains 51 selectable Ontario hospital/health-network names. This is a manually checked snapshot, not a live Google Places integration or a claim of province-wide completeness.

## Interpretation and limits

- London Health Sciences Centre is a distinct Maps listing at 800 Commissioners Road East, London, alongside its campus names. It is now directly selectable. University Hospital also includes the LHSC name.
- Google Maps lists Victoria Hospital and Children’s Hospital together. Both existing hospital names remain searchable/selectable because the official LHSC directory identifies both services. They refer to the same campus.
- McMaster University Medical Centre resolves to the Hamilton Health Sciences listing at 1200 Main Street West; the children’s hospital has its own listing at that address. This is a campus-level match, not an exact Maps name match.
- Thunder Bay Regional Health Sciences Centre was corroborated through Maps hospital, emergency and cancer-care listings at 980 Oliver Road, linked to tbrhsc.net. Department listings were not added as separate hospitals.
- Current Maps names Hennick Humber Hospital, Southlake Health and Waterloo Regional Health Network are displayed with familiar former names available as search aliases.
- Toronto includes North York, East York and Scarborough; Ottawa includes Nepean. These district names are also searchable.
- Search results may contain duplicate or departmental listings. Matching names/locations and linked hospital websites were used, not ratings or reviews. No names outside Ontario were added.
