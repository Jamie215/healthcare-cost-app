# Ontario hospital suggestions

This is a bundled Ontario prototype directory, not an exhaustive or live registry. See [Google Maps verification](hospital-maps-verification.md) for the September 18, 2026 cross-check and expansion to 51 entries. It contains Ontario hospitals only. Names and locations were checked against official sources on September 17, 2026. Users may enter an unlisted hospital manually. Searching happens locally; typed names are not transmitted.

- [Ontario hospital locations](https://www.ontario.ca/page/general-hospital-locations): Brampton, Markham, Pembroke and Hawkesbury. The search index was accessible; the full page returned 403.
- [Ontario hospital classifications](https://www.ontario.ca/page/classification-hospitals): Kingston General and Children's Hospital of Eastern Ontario.
- [University Health Network](https://www.uhn.ca/OurHospitals): Toronto General, Toronto Western and Princess Margaret.
- [Sinai Health](https://www.sinaihealth.ca/about-sinai-health/contact-sinai-health): Mount Sinai and Hennick Bridgepoint.
- [SickKids](https://www.sickkids.ca/en/about/contact/)
- [Sunnybrook](https://sunnybrook.ca/contact-us/)
- [Trillium Health Partners](https://www.thp.ca/ineed/directions/Pages/default.aspx): Credit Valley and Mississauga.
- [Hamilton Health Sciences](https://www.hamiltonhealthsciences.ca/patients-visitors/visiting-hhs/): Hamilton General, Juravinski and McMaster sites.
- [The Ottawa Hospital](https://www.ottawahospital.on.ca/fr/patients-et-visiteurs/itineraires-et-cartes): General, Civic and Riverside campuses.
- [London Health Sciences Centre](https://www.lhsc.on.ca/about-lhsc/welcome): University, Victoria and Children's hospitals.
